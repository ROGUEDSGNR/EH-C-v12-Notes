# **Footprinting Countermeasures**

1. **Restrict Access**:
   - <mark>Restrict employees</mark>' access to social networking sites from the organization's network.

2. **Web Server Configuration**:
   - <mark>Configure web servers</mark> to prevent information leakage.

3. **Employee Education**:
   - Educate employees to <mark>use pseudonyms</mark> on blogs, groups, and forums.
   - Conduct <mark>regular security awareness training</mark> to educate employees about various social engineering tricks and risks.
   - Train employees to<mark> counter social engineering</mark> techniques and attacks.

4. **Information Control**:
   - Avoid revealing critical information in <mark>press releases, annual reports, product catalogs</mark>, etc.
   - <mark>Limit the amount of information</mark> published on the website or the Internet.

5. **Footprinting and Removal**:
   - Use <mark>footprinting techniques</mark> to identify and remove any publicly available sensitive information.

6. **Search Engine and Registration Controls**:
   - Prevent search engines from caching web pages and <mark>use anonymous registration services</mark>.
   - Use <mark>privacy services</mark> on a Whois lookup database.
   - Request archive.org to <mark>delete the website's history</mark> from the archive database.
   - Keep the <mark>domain name profile private</mark>.
   - <mark>Sanitize details provided</mark> to Internet registrars to hide the organization's direct contact information.

7. **Security Policies**:
   - <mark>Develop and enforce security policies</mark>, such as information security and password policies, to regulate what employees can reveal to third parties.

8. **DNS Configuration**:
   - Separate internal and external DNS or use split DNS, and <mark>restrict zone transfers</mark> to authorized servers.

9. **Web Server Security**:
   - <mark>Disable directory listings</mark> on web servers.
   - Configure Internet Information Services (IIS) to <mark>avoid information disclosure through banner grabbing</mark>.

10. **Geolocation Controls**:
   - Disable the geo-tagging functionality on cameras to <mark>prevent geolocation tracking</mark>.
   -  <mark>Avoid revealing location or travel plans </mark>on social networking sites.
   - <mark>Turn off geolocation access</mark> on all mobile devices when not required.

11. **Sensitive Information Handling**:
   - <mark>Encrypt and password-protect sensitive information</mark>.
   - <mark>Store critical documents</mark>, such as business plans and proprietary documents, <mark>offline to prevent exploitation</mark>.

12. **Protocol and IP Security**:
   - <mark>Disable unnecessary protocols</mark>.
   -  <mark>Always use TCP/IP and IPsec filters</mark> for defense in depth.
   - <mark>Hide the IP address</mark> and related information <mark>by implementing a VPN</mark> or placing the server behind a secure proxy.

13. **Employee Account Management**:
   - <mark>Disable or delete the accounts of employees who have left</mark> the organization.

14. **Mail Server Configuration**:
   - <mark>Configure mail servers to ignore emails from anonymous individuals</mark>.

15. **Internal Communication Security**:
   - <mark>Ensure no critical information</mark>, such as strategic plans, product information, or sales projections, <mark>is displayed on notice boards or walls</mark>.
